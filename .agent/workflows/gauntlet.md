# Gauntlet — Artsy edition

Every package and site declares a named external reference and one compared property. A reference without a property is a mood board; a property without a reference is self-assessment. Lop write bubbles and PM-owned contracts remain in force.

## Roles and loop

PM defines a small separately judgeable visitor task and its bar. A builder implements inside its bubble. A **fresh, separately spawned critic** receives only the task, the domain spec/bar, rules and artifact location. Do not supply the builder's reasoning, history or report. Reusing the builder under a critic heading is not independence.

The critic opens the real artifact, performs the task and compares the named property with a reachable reference. It returns one largest gap, evidence and a decision: pass, needs-work, blocked, or PM-stopped. The critic never fixes the code. Builder addresses the gap, then another round runs. No fixed number of rounds. Stop only at an argued pass or a recorded PM stop with the remaining gap visible.

## Artifacts by domain

UI: `sites/demo/out`, served and rendered—not source files or screenshots alone. Query: executed path output plus assertion proof opened in the viewer. Sources: raw snapshot, normalization diff and actual replay result. Identity: review queue, decision ledger and reversible merge replay. Chronology/geography: real ambiguous-date/place cases in the rendered lens and their sources. Media: rendered object image, source identity, rights evidence, keyboard alternative and failed-image state. Learning: a served lesson and a documented learner task. A contract-only package has no built artifact and cannot pass a product Gauntlet.

Use 390x844 and 1440x900, keyboard-only navigation, back/reload and at least one screen reader. Inspect DOM headings, links, focus order, status announcements and source provenance. Compare beside the reference, blind A/B when feasible. A pass explains why the chosen property meets the bar and names the evidence, not a star rating. A dozen unrelated findings is not a focused round.

If the artifact or reference cannot be reached, say blocked after trying it. Do not substitute a builder report. Screenshots of missing-image fallbacks do not establish image quality. Test success does not establish historical truth, pedagogy, coverage or a rights clearance.

## Review output

Store `reviews/<wave>/<domain>-round-N.md`: task; reviewer/session identity; artifact revision and URL; reference URL and access result; actions performed; one largest gap; evidence files/claim IDs; verdict and argument. PM alone records a stop or structural ruling. Do not fill this with fictional reviewers. M0's `reviews/2026-10-28.md` is a builder verification report, explicitly not a critic pass.

## Whole-product and onboarding gates

At the end of a wave, a fresh reviewer checks consistency of vocabulary, uncertainty labels, controls and source presentation across domains. At a milestone, a fresh builder mounts a throwaway Edo-only site using the documented APIs. Every required package edit fails the onboarding drill and becomes a prioritized gap. Existing Italian assumptions must not pass just because one demo looks coherent.

Done means checks/tests/architecture/build, source and rights gates, plus an argued independent critic pass or explicit PM stop. Missing subagent capability must be reported, not simulated.
